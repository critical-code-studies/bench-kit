# An Example Text

## Chapter 1. Contingency [コマ 3-4]

[p. 1] [コマ 3] Contingency, it is said, is what is not necessary. What is necessary could not be otherwise; what is contingent could.[^1]

That a thing could be otherwise is its [p. 2] possibility, and contingency is bound to possibility.

## Chapter 2. Encounter [コマ 4]

[p. 3] [コマ 4] Two series meet: this is the encounter, and the encounter is contingency at its ground.

[^1]: The source's 偶然 is rendered contingency throughout.
