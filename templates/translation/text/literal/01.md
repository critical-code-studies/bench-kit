# An Example Text, literal

## Chapter 1. Chance (偶然) [コマ 3-4]

[p. 1] [コマ 3] Chance (偶然) is, as it is said, what is not necessary. Where a thing is necessary, it could not be otherwise; where a thing is by chance, it could be otherwise.[^1]

That a thing could be otherwise is its [p. 2] possibility (可能性). Chance is therefore bound to possibility.

## Chapter 2. Encounter (邂逅) [コマ 4]

[p. 3] [コマ 4] Two series meet: this is the encounter (邂逅). The encounter is chance in its ground (根據).

[^1]: The source writes 偶然 throughout; see the glossary.
